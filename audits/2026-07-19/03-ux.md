# UX Audit — Houses of Thought

**Auditor:** ux-reviewer (subagent, model: Fable) · read-only · 2026-07-19

Explored the full repo (Next.js 16 + Supabase app: marketing site, `/try` Mini
House, auth, dashboard, 7-layer build workspace, and teacher/student classroom
flows). Findings ordered by severity.

## Critical

**C1. Primary conversion CTAs link to routes that don't exist (404s).**
There is no `/signup` route (signup lives at `/login?mode=signup`), yet:
- `components/sections/FinalCtaSection.tsx:39` — homepage final CTA → `/signup`
- `components/sections/EducatorHeroSection.tsx:55` and `app/educators/page.tsx:30` — the educators page hero **and** bottom CTA ("Create a classroom") → `/signup?role=educator`. The educator funnel — the strategic wedge — dead-ends twice on its own landing page, and the `role=educator` intent is never consumed anywhere even if the route existed.

Friction: a motivated teacher clicks the one button built for them and gets a 404. Recommendation: point all of these at `/login?mode=signup` (and pre-select the Teacher account type from a `role` param), or add a `/signup` route that renders the signup tab.

**C2. A whole family of referenced pages doesn't exist.**
Routes referenced but absent from `app/`:
- `/framework` — Header mobile menu (`Header.tsx:280`), Footer, `DashboardHeader.tsx:83` (center nav slot of every authenticated page), `app/how-it-works/page.tsx:30` CTA, `app/story/page.tsx:24` CTA, and copy "Full model at /framework" (`HowBuildFlowSection.tsx:151`)
- `/contact` — Footer, mobile menu, educators "Talk to us" secondary CTA
- `/terms`, `/privacy` — Footer "Legal" column, and worse, `EducatorTrustSection.tsx:64-65` links them from the privacy/trust pitch aimed at schools **(content exists at `legal/*.md`, just unrouted)**
- `/forgot-password` — `app/login/page.tsx:256`

Friction: broken Terms/Privacy inside a trust section actively damages credibility with the school audience; a broken "Forgot password?" locks users out permanently. Recommendation: ship minimal `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/contact`, `/framework` pages and wire Supabase password reset; until then remove the links.

**C3. The build workspace simulates core actions with fake success feedback.**
In `lib/build/state.ts`:
- `SEND_INVITE` → toast "Invite sent to {name}" — no email, no record, nothing sent
- `COPY_LINK` → toast "Invite link copied" — **nothing is written to the clipboard** (no `navigator.clipboard` call anywhere in the build flow)
- `PUBLISH` → toast "House published · strength N" — nothing is published; this is also the final step's primary CTA ("Publish house", `Canvas.tsx` footer) and the ContextBar's top-right primary button
- `EXPORT` → toast "Exported as PDF" — no PDF

Plus fabricated social proof: every user's ContextBar shows a presence stack of fake collaborators "Maya R." and "Devan K." (`lib/build/people.ts`, `ContextBar.tsx:158-168`) with seeded presence ("In Evidence") and a fake activity feed. Friction: users believe they've invited classmates, shared links, and published work; none of it happened. This is the most trust-destroying pattern in the product. Recommendation: until collaboration/publish ship, remove the fake avatars and gate Invite/Publish behind honest "Coming soon" affordances — never a success toast for a no-op.

**C4. The try → signup bridge drops the user's work, contradicting its own promise.**
`MiniHouseResult.tsx:294` builds `signupHref = /login?mode=signup&q=<question>` and the copy says "Mini House results aren't saved. Create an account to keep them." But `app/login/page.tsx` reads only `mode` and `next` — the `q` param is discarded, and post-signup the user lands on an empty dashboard with no house, no question, no tour. Friction: the single highest-intent moment in the funnel (just saw value, willing to sign up) ends in a cold empty screen and a broken promise. Recommendation: carry `q` through signup, auto-create a house with `question` prefilled (or persist the Mini House itself), and land the user in `/build/[id]` — not `/dashboard`.

## High

**H1. The build workspace has zero responsive handling.**
`BuildHousePage.tsx` hard-codes a three-zone layout: 264px blueprint rail + canvas + 320px right rail, `height: 100vh`, `overflow: hidden`. `app/globals.css` contains no media queries for any build, dashboard, or classroom component (the only responsive rules are for marketing/Mini House grids). On a phone the workspace is ~600px of chrome before content. Marketing pages are fully responsive, so mobile visitors convert into an app they cannot use. Recommendation: below ~900px collapse the rails into a bottom sheet/drawer and tabs; at minimum show a designed "best on a larger screen" interstitial rather than a broken layout.

**H2. Navigation dead-end inside the workspace.**
`components/build/AppBar.tsx`: the logo/wordmark is a plain `<div>` (not a link), and "Framework" / "Collab" are inert `<span>`s styled exactly like nav items. There is no route back to `/dashboard` from `/build/[id]` other than the browser back button. Recommendation: make the logo link to `/dashboard`, remove or wire the dead nav spans.

**H3. "Collab" nav item silently creates database rows → Untitled House clutter.**
`DashboardHeader.tsx:103` labels the link "Collab" but it points at `/build`, which (`app/build/page.tsx`) immediately inserts a blank house and redirects. Every curious click mints another "Untitled House" card with "No question set yet · 0/7 layers", which the dashboard then displays forever. This is the classic empty-clutter anti-pattern the dashboard will drown in. Recommendation: rename the item ("New house" or remove it — the dashboard already has Create New House), and either create the row lazily on first edit or auto-garbage-collect never-touched empty houses.

**H4. No first-run experience anywhere; the promised tour doesn't exist.**
- New-user dashboard: heading + a dashed Create tile; no explanation of what a house is or a pointer to examples.
- `ProfileForm.tsx:174-190`: an "Onboarding Tour" settings card with a "Restart tour" button whose only effect is the message "The guided tour is on the way." — a control for a feature that has never existed.
- `app/welcome/page.tsx` is a literal placeholder (eyebrow text: "Placeholder") that nothing links to, and it routes to `/build` (see H3).
Recommendation: delete the tour card and `/welcome` until real; give the empty dashboard a designed first-run card ("Start from your question" + example link).

**H5. House "Share (copy link)" copies a link nobody else can open.**
`HouseCard.tsx:49-60` copies `/build/<id>`, but `app/build/[id]/page.tsx` + RLS bounce any non-owner (except a teacher of that student) to their own dashboard. The menu item says "Share" and confirms "Link copied" — recipients silently hit a dead end. Recommendation: remove Share until a public/read-only view exists, or copy a link to a real shared render.

**H6. Signup edge cases unhandled.**
`app/login/page.tsx`: if Supabase email confirmation is enabled, `signUp` returns without a session, the `profiles` update fails, and `router.push('/dashboard')` bounces straight back to login with no message ("check your email" state does not exist). Error copy is raw Supabase text (`result.error.message`). Password rules exist only as `minLength` + placeholder "Min. 8 characters". Recommendation: add an explicit post-signup confirmation state and translate common auth errors into human copy.

## Medium

**M1. The teaching framework doesn't map consistently across surfaces.**
Three different decompositions of the same method: marketing "How it works" teaches **5 steps** over a **6-layer** house diagram (`HowBuildFlowSection.tsx`); the builder is **7 layers** (Frame → Perspectives → Evidence → Assumptions → Conclusion → Implications → Review, `lib/build/content.ts`); the Mini House renders **5 sections** in a *different order* with different names ("Synthesis" vs "Conclusion", "Sub-conclusions" appearing only there). A student moving from marketing → try → build meets three taxonomies. Recommendation: pick the 7-layer names as canonical and re-map the marketing steps and Mini House section labels onto them ("Layer 5 · Conclusion" etc.).

**M2. Internal jargon leaks into user-facing chrome.**
- `SheetStrip.tsx`: every page carries "Method · Trapasso / Paul–Elder · Rev. A", "Sheet 00 / Try it", footer "Sheet 99 / Footer" — blueprint-flavored, but meaningless labels to a first-time user (and "Trapasso" is unexplained anywhere).
- `ProfileForm.tsx:161`: "Element 4.2 · These perspectives persist across all your houses" — internal framework numbering.
- The workspace's only help affordance is a "What's new" drawer (`WhatsNewDrawer.tsx`) explaining changes "vs the old flow" — a design-handoff artifact; no user has seen an old flow. Recommendation: replace the drawer with actual layer help; cut the element/sheet numbering or make it purely decorative.

**M3. Two overlapping "try" surfaces, one orphaned, with contradictory copy.**
`/try` = Mini House (not saved); `/house` = full builder with localStorage persistence, reachable from nowhere. Meanwhile the sitewide CTA note (`app/examples/page.tsx:103`, CTASection `note`) says "No sign-up needed to try it. **Your work saves locally** until you create an account" — which describes the orphaned `/house`, not `/try`, whose own copy says results aren't saved. Recommendation: either link `/house` as "keep building without an account" after the Mini House, or delete it and fix the note.

**M4. Learn/Decide mode toggle is undiscoverable and unexplained.**
`ContextBar.tsx:123-154`: two 10px uppercase mono buttons ("LEARN | DECIDE") whose only explanation is a native `title` tooltip ("How much help the co-pilot gives."). It meaningfully changes the AI's behavior (Socratic questions vs actionable suggestions, `CopilotPanel.tsx:241-249`) but nothing in the UI teaches this at the moment of choice. Recommendation: label it ("Co-pilot: Learn / Decide") and add a one-line explanation in the co-pilot intro tile, which currently doesn't mention mode.

**M5. Joining a class gives no confirmation and lands in the wrong place.**
`app/join/[code]/page.tsx` redeems the code then `router.replace('/dashboard')` — the dashboard shows houses, not classes; the joined class is only visible on `/classes`. No "You joined Ms. X's class" moment. Recommendation: redirect students to `/classes` with a success notice naming the class.

**M6. Empty house scoring reads as failure.**
A brand-new house shows "House strength 0/100" in red (ContextBar) and "score 0" in the rail before the user has typed a word. First feedback from the product is a failing grade. Recommendation: show "—" or "Not scored yet" until at least one layer has content.

**M7. Destructive delete without undo.** `HouseCard.tsx` Delete → "Confirm delete" inside the same kebab menu (two clicks, 14px-tall menu rows) permanently deletes a house with all layers; no undo, no typed confirmation for houses with real content. The account DeleteAccountModal exists, but house deletion is comparatively cheap. Recommendation: toast with undo window, or a modal when the house is non-empty.

**M8. `/classes` is missing from `middleware.ts` `PROTECTED_PREFIXES`** — logged-out visitors render the page shell and get a client-side bounce to plain `/login` without a `?next=` (unlike every other protected route). Minor inconsistency; add it to the list.

## Low

**L1. Hover-only affordances, no focus styles.** Nearly all interactive styling is inline `onMouseEnter/onMouseLeave`; there are no `:focus-visible` rules in `globals.css`, so keyboard users get browser-default (or no) focus indication across the app; nav "links" in AppBar aren't focusable at all.

**L2. Tiny touch targets and microcopy.** Kebab button 28×28px (`HouseCard.tsx:187`), mode toggle ~26px tall, 9–10px uppercase mono meta text throughout the dashboard/workspace — below the 44px/legibility bar on touch devices (compounded by H1).

**L3. Loading states are bare centered mono text** ("LOADING YOUR HOUSES…") for dashboard, classroom, build load — versus the genuinely well-designed staged loader on `/try` and skeleton cards in the co-pilot. Inconsistent quality of the same state across the app.

**L4. Rate-limit copy is charming but uninformative.** "The co-pilot is resting — daily limit reached. It resets tomorrow." (`lib/ai/findings.ts`) never says what the limit is or that it's account-wide; fine, but consider a count-down or limit hint.

**L5. Fake loading checklist on `/try`.** `MINI_HOUSE_BUILD_STAGES` is a cosmetic client-side timer, not real progress. It's a defensible pattern and well-executed — flagging only because the brand promise is "reasoning you can inspect."

## What's genuinely good
The `/try` flow is the strongest surface: zero-friction entry, example chips, char counter, cmd+enter, minimum-display loading, designed error state with retry/reset, and a persuasive comparison table. The co-pilot panel has real loading/error/empty/stale states. `ProfileForm` has honest save/error indicators. The join-code flow preserves `?next=` through login. Keep these as the quality bar.

## Changes most likely to improve activation, in order
1. **Fix the broken conversion links** (C1, C2): `/signup`, educator CTAs, forgot-password, terms/privacy. Cheapest, highest-leverage.
2. **Carry the Mini House question through signup into a pre-filled house** and land in `/build/[id]` (C4) — turns the best moment in the funnel into the first meaningful action.
3. **Remove the fake collaboration/publish/toasts and fake avatars** (C3) — protects the trust the whole product is selling.
4. **Give the dashboard a real first-run state and stop minting Untitled Houses from nav** (H3, H4).
5. **Make the workspace navigable and honest at small sizes** (H1, H2): logo → dashboard, responsive collapse or a designed small-screen interstitial.
6. **Unify the framework vocabulary** across marketing, Mini House, and the 7 layers (M1) so the teaching model reinforces, rather than fights, the build flow.
