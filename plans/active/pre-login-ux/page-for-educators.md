# Page — For Educators (`/educators`)

**Goal:** convert teachers. This is the **primary wedge** page — its own hero,
voice, and CTA aimed at educators, not individuals.

**Primary CTA:** Create a classroom (`/signup?role=teacher`).
**Secondary:** Try it free · See an example assignment.

## Sections

### 1. Educator hero
- Eyebrow `SHEET / FOR EDUCATORS`. Serif Display L: *"Make critical thinking
  visible."* Sub: *"Houses of Thought gives your students a structure for reasoning
  — and gives you a window into how they think, not just what they answered."*
- CTAs: `Create a classroom` (primary) · `Try it yourself` (secondary).
- Visual: teacher's-eye view — a roster of student houses with strength scores.

### 2. The problem teachers feel
- Short copy: chatbots let students skip the thinking; critical thinking is hard to
  *see* and harder to grade. Three pain points as compact items.

### 3. How classrooms work
- **Layout:** 3 `StepCard`s: **Create a class & invite students** (link/code) →
  **Assign a question or let them choose** → **Review their houses** (see evidence,
  assumptions, perspectives, strength — the reasoning, not just the verdict).

### 4. The student mode difference  ← key differentiator
- **Layout:** comparison (`Tabs` or two cards) of account modes:
  - **Student:** structured builder, Research Mode, Logic Strength, Stress Test —
    **AI assistant steps back** so the reasoning stays the student's own.
  - **Teacher / Standard:** full access including the Collab AI co-reasoning
    assistant.
- **Copy hook:** "The AI won't do their homework. That's the point."
- **Tradeoff — leading with a *restriction* as a feature:** unusual, but it's
  exactly the trust signal educators want; we frame the restriction as pedagogy,
  not a limitation.

### 5. Multi-user / collaboration (classroom-only)
- Brief: real collaboration (teacher↔student, peer review) lives **in classrooms**
  — distinct from the individual Collab (human+AI) builder. Only claim what exists;
  mark anything roadmap as such.

### 6. Trust & safety
- Compact: age floor 12+, privacy posture (work is private, no selling data),
  student data handling. Link Privacy/Terms. (Flag COPPA/FERPA review as a build
  task; copy here must match final policy.)

### 7. CTA
- Dark `CTASection`: `Create a classroom` + `Talk to us` (→ `/contact`).

## Responsive
- Comparison tabs collapse to stacked cards `<md`.
- Roster visual scales; on `sm` show 2–3 rows, not a full grid.

## A11y / notes
- Account-mode comparison readable as plain text (not color-only).
- Keep claims synced with the real product (student AI disabled; multi-user =
  classrooms only).
