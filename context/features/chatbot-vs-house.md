# Chatbot vs. House — Core Differentiator

The central pitch of the product. Every public-facing page should reinforce this
contrast directly or indirectly.

## The problem with chatbot answers

A chatbot delivers a confident paragraph — but hides everything behind it. The
user cannot see:

- **Assumptions** — what was taken for granted without evidence
- **Evidence** — what sources (if any) the answer rests on
- **Perspectives** — whose viewpoint was represented and whose was skipped
- **Logic** — how the conclusion was reached from the premises

The result is undefensible. The user can't check the reasoning, challenge it, or
learn from it. Worse, the chatbot *sounds* certain even when it's wrong.

**Example portrayal (homepage):** The chatbot card shows a one-line assistant
reply — *"Yes, you should definitely do it. It's the best option."* — with a
warning: "No assumptions · No sources · No way to check." The brevity and
false certainty are the point.

## What a house gives you instead

A house is a layered reasoning structure built bottom-up. Every layer is
visible, named, and open to inspection:

| Layer | What it holds |
|---|---|
| 01 Concepts | Shared vocabulary for the topic |
| 02 Question | The central question being answered |
| 03 Perspectives | Three lenses: Self, Group, Ideas |
| 04 Evidence | Cited sources gathered in Research Mode |
| 05 Assumptions | Beliefs taken as true without direct evidence |
| 06 Conclusion | Synthesized answer with a strength score |
| 07 Implications | Positive, negative, and uncertain consequences |

The conclusion comes with a **House Strength score** (Evidence / Logic /
Coverage) so you can see exactly where it's strong and where it's weak.

## Why this matters for positioning

- **For educators:** the house makes critical thinking visible and gradeable.
  A chatbot answer is a black box; a house is a transparent structure a teacher
  can discuss, annotate, and assess layer by layer.
- **For individuals:** you learn *how* to reason, not just *what* the answer is.
  The AI guides via questions (Collab) rather than handing you a verdict.
- **Against competitors:** every AI writing tool produces output. Houses of
  Thought produces *defensible reasoning* — a fundamentally different artifact.
