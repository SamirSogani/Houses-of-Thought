# Houses of Thought — Handoff

## What this is

An AI-powered structured reasoning platform on Trapasso's House of Reason model —
users build layered "houses" from question to defensible conclusion, with the AI as a
Socratic partner rather than an answer engine. See
[vision/product-strategy.md](vision/product-strategy.md).
