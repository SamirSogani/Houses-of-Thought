## `/about`

The real origin story, told warmly: a parent building a way to carry a
real teacher's classroom framework anywhere, instead of it living only on
paper in one classroom. Clear credit to John Trapasso and to the
Paul-Elder framework he draws on.

**Privacy default:** this draft keeps the story at the level of "a real
teacher's real classroom framework" without naming the son, his age, or
his school — a minor's details don't belong in marketing copy by
default. Add specifics only if you're explicitly comfortable with them
being public.
