Correction to my first pass: I designed a *new* demo. There isn't a gap to
fill — `/try` already drops visitors into the real Collab builder, no login,
work saved to `localStorage`, imported on signup (`decisions/001` #6). The
job here is narrower: make sure marketing pages accurately preview it, and
that nothing implies a mechanic the product doesn't have.

## What a real result actually contains (`components/try/MiniHouseResult.tsx`)

Not a pro/con list. Not a verdict. In order:

1. **Restated question**, with the original phrasing kept alongside it.
2. **Three perspectives** — fixed lenses: Practical/Logical, Emotional/
   Personal, Long-Term/Strategic — each with a summary and its own
   sub-questions answered in turn.
3. **Sub-conclusions** — what each perspective concludes, on its own, before
   anything is merged.
4. **Assumptions** — labeled by type.
5. **Evidence** — numbered, sourced, each with a real citation.
6. **Synthesis** — a final take, key tradeoffs, the strongest tension — and it
   closes on a **reflective question**, not a lean or a verdict. This is the
   detail my first pass got backwards (I had it end on "leans toward: take it,
   63/37," which is exactly the AI-hands-out-a-verdict pattern the product
   deliberately refuses to do).

Then the real conversion block: *"This was the compressed version"* → **Create
free account** (href carries the original question through signup) or **Try
another question** — with an honest reassurance line, *"Mini House results
aren't saved. Create an account to keep them,"* and a real side-by-side
(Mini House: 3 perspectives, 6 sub-questions, 3 assumptions, sourced evidence,
single synthesis, not saved — vs. a free account: unlimited/customizable
perspectives, full assumption taxonomy including unknown unknowns, cited
research, an AI co-pilot that challenges your reasoning, House Strength +
stress tests, autosaved).

## What this means for the marketing pages

Every place my first pass showed a generic "sample result" (home's example
teaser, the demo preview) should show *this* shape instead — three named
perspectives and a reflective close, not a pro/con card with a lean. It's more
distinctive than what I invented, and it's real, which matters more.

## Still true from my first pass

Framing `/try` as the primary CTA everywhere, "no sign-up to try" as a
recurring reassurance line, and treating the moment someone runs their own
question as the single highest-leverage moment on the site.
