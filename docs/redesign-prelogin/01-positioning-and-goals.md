# Positioning & Goals

[← Back to index](README.md)

## Why this redesign

Houses of Thought is becoming a free, standalone alternative to paid AI
decision-making tools — most immediately relevant because Rationale (by
Jina AI) is reportedly winding down. See
[10-open-questions.md](10-open-questions.md) for a name correction and an
unverified-claim flag on that — the positioning below is written to hold
up either way.

## Who this is for

Two audiences arrive pre-login, and the site needs to serve both without
diluting either:

1. **Someone with a real decision in front of them** — a job offer, a
   move, a purchase, a hard conversation, a civic choice. They want more
   rigor than a pros/cons list but no patience for a steep learning curve.
   This is the primary audience for `/`, `/try`, `/method`, `/why-free`.
2. **Education-adjacent visitors** — teachers, students, parents — who
   recognize the Paul-Elder / critical-thinking framing. They should feel
   welcomed and quickly routed to the existing **for-educators** experience
   rather than served a parallel pitch here.

## Positioning pillars

1. **Real methodology, not a prompt trick.** Grounded in Paul and Elder's
   published critical-thinking framework and a real teacher's
   classroom-tested structure — not "GPT wrapped in a nice UI."
2. **It doesn't stop at pros and cons.** A full pass through all eight
   elements of reasoning, each checked against nine standards, looping
   until the reasoning would hold up under real scrutiny — not a single
   AI response.
3. **Free.** No paywall on the core experience. (Exact sustainability
   model — fully free, freemium, mission-subsidized — is still open; see
   open questions.)
4. **Rigor without the homework.** The intellectual foundation is
   academic; the experience should feel like it was built for a phone in
   your pocket at 11pm, not a term paper.

## What "done" looks like

- A first-time visitor understands the differentiator within ~10 seconds
  on the landing page.
- A visitor can experience one real result (via the live demo) without
  creating an account.
- The methodology reads as credible and considered, never gimmicky.
- Nobody describes the motion/graphics as confusing — "memorable" and
  "clear" are not in tension anywhere on the site.

## Explicit non-goals

- Not touching anything post-login.
- Not touching the for-educators route.
- Not changing the underlying reasoning methodology or AI behavior — this
  is presentation and experience only.
- Not shipping code yet — this pass is design only.
